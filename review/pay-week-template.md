# Pay week company summary

Use this for every future Noble Field Ledger pay week. The company summary is the first thing on the review page. Copy this layout. Recompute the cells for that week. Call every person a contractor.

## Columns

Company | Hours | Wages | Assurity | Subtotal | Fuel/mileage/reimbursement | Thanksgiving bonus | Piecework bonus | Additional lines | Net payable

## Rows

WD, GC, then the week total. The week-total row is labeled **Week**.

The entire WD company row is blue `#7eb6e0`. GC is not blue. Week is the total row.

## Formulas

- Subtotal = wages minus the Assurity deduction. Assurity is shown as a minus, so subtotal is wages plus that negative number.
- Net payable = subtotal + fuel/mileage/reimbursement + Thanksgiving bonus + piecework bonus + additional lines.
- Week net payable is the figure Kelly pays from.
- A zero amount in these columns is `$0.00`.
- Week hours are the WD hours plus the GC hours already on the page. Do not convert that week total again.

## Where a contractor’s amounts go

A contractor with WD hours and zero GC hours: fuel/mileage/reimbursement, Thanksgiving bonus, piecework bonus, and Assurity go on the WD row. Eric Havins is always WD, including every hour and his Assurity.

A contractor with GC hours and zero WD hours: those amounts go on the GC row.

A contractor with no hours stays in the week total only and is named under the table. Matthew Laush is that contractor for 11/16/2026. His Thanksgiving bonus is in the week Thanksgiving total and on neither company row.

Mixed WD and GC hours are not split across both rows. Name that contractor under the table. Those amounts stay in the week total only.

26-GC hours are GC hours. Do not show a separate 26-GC total, column, or subtotal.

## Assurity

Assurity is always a deduction, never an addition, and only for enrolled contractors. Show every Assurity amount as a minus. A contractor who is not enrolled stays at `$0.00`. Do not invent an Assurity amount.

## Additional lines

Additional lines are pay that is not WD wages and not GC wages. They are `$0.00` on the WD row and `$0.00` on the GC row. They sit on the Week row only.

For 11/16/2026 they are Exceptional Cleaning Services/Paulina Moya `$157.50` and Angel Soto - Helper Esias `$310.50`.

## Mileage

Company vehicle mileage is `$0.60` per mile. That is Nicholas Sturgeon and Matthew Laush. Personal truck is `$0.76`. That is Marco Garcia when the odometer is under 300,000. A company truck over 300,000 miles is `$0.60`.

## Blue rows

Blue `#7eb6e0` is on the entire WD company row, and on every contractor row and every WD job row for contractors paid only through Windows & Doors. The legend reads: Blue rows are paid only through Windows & Doors.

## Hours

Per-punch tenths use the leftover minutes:

| Leftover minutes | Tenth |
| --- | --- |
| 00–05 | .0 |
| 06–11 | .1 |
| 12–17 | .2 |
| 18–23 | .3 |
| 24–29 | .4 |
| 30–35 | .5 |
| 36–41 | .6 |
| 42–47 | .7 |
| 48–53 | .8 |
| 54–59 | .9 |

Convert each punch, then add those tenths. Do not convert the week total again.

## Not calculated

George Piekarski and Nicholas Sommer are not calculated when they have no rate. Their hours are in neither the WD total nor the GC total.
